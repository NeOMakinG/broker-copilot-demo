# Konsileo: product and market notes

Independent research from public sources, October 2026. Not affiliated with Konsileo.

## The business
Konsileo is a UK chartered commercial insurance broker founded in 2017 by John Warburton (CEO) and Peter Henderson (COO) [2]. It arranges more than £85 million in annual premiums for UK businesses, from construction and healthcare to technology and professional services, with over 220 people working remotely across the country [2][6]. Revenue is brokerage commission on placed policies. The model stands apart from most peers: brokers are self-managed with no branch network or middle management, and growth comes from recruiting brokers one by one rather than buying books of business [4]. In October 2026 the firm closed a £5 million (€5.9M) equity round led by existing investors Committed Capital and ACF Investors, earmarked for hiring brokers and integrating AI into its platform [2][3]. Earlier raises: £2.7M in 2018 and £8M of debt and equity in November 2024 [2].

## Market
- Global insurance brokerage was worth $335.9 billion in 2025, with forecast growth of 9.6% a year through 2033 [4].
- UK commercial broking is consolidating: PE-backed groups such as PIB (57 acquisitions to date) and Ardonagh keep absorbing regional brokers [15].
- Konsileo bets against the rollup: proprietary software that makes each broker more productive, with premium growth compounding organically [4].

## Product notes
- The core asset is a proprietary, in-house broking platform that automates admin, embeds compliance checks and captures structured risk data; the company says it cuts process effort by half [2][5].
- Riskube, the client portal, gives policyholders policy access, risk insights and direct messaging with their broker; trade press describes it as one of the only client-facing platforms of its kind among UK commercial brokers [5][10].
- An AI-supported peer file-review process for compliance reduced compliance errors by 36% and was shortlisted for Excellence in AI at the 2025 Insurance Times Awards [8][9].
- Recognition includes Commercial Lines Broker of the Year 2025, Best Insurance Employer SME 2025 and a Sunday Times Best Places to Work 2026 award [6][9].
- Public client feedback is thin: 3.5 on Trustpilot from 5 reviews; employee satisfaction is strong at 4.7 on Glassdoor from 30 reviews [11][12].
- Site content is uneven, with headcount ranging from "over 70" to 220+ depending on the page, and there is no public documentation for the platform itself [5][7].

## Opportunities
- The new round explicitly funds AI integration [2]: risk-intake summarisation, submission drafting and insurer appetite matching are natural next steps for a platform that already captures structured risk data.
- Compliance AI has a proven result (36% fewer errors) [9]; applying the same file-matching approach before submission would push quality upstream and cut rework with insurers.
- Riskube could gain client-side document upload and renewal-pack assembly, closing the gap with digital-first competitors on self-service [10][13].
- A remote workforce of 220+ self-managed brokers creates demand for cohort onboarding and coaching analytics inside the platform [2][7].
